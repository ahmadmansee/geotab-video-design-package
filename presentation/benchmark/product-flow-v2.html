02 / 06 · Motive · Events

Event preview, list, and detail

Safety preview → full list → event detail

→ →
+

What and who: The preview shows behavior and driver. The full list adds time, risk labels, and filters.

−

Hard to rank: No severity in the preview. The visible full-list rows all say “Medium Risk,” and the open filter covers the list.

−

Driver context comes later: “Critical” names the risk and “Pending Review” names the work state above the video. The driver's name is below the video, so these details are not together.

05 / 06 · Samsara · Fleet

Fleet map and vehicle detail

→
+

Calm layout: The map and vehicle sheet have good white space. Vehicle ID, speed, driver, and address are easy to find.

Weak hierarchy: Filter options hide behind a small icon beside “Showing 10 assets,” away from search. In the vehicle sheet, “Driving” is small, while the driver, address, and Diagnostics rows have similar weight.
