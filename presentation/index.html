The problem

A busy fleet safety manager needs to see what needs attention at a glance.

Is my fleet safe right now, and who should I worry about?

Hundreds of events arrive each day. Only a few need review.

Managers may check in between other tasks, away from a desk.

Camera images and video may load slowly on a weak connection.

Benchmarks

We looked at Motive and Samsara’s mobile screens for fleet status, events, cameras, and drivers.

04 / 15

Fleet and cameras

  • 01Rounded cards give the page breathing room, though the map takes most of the screen.
  • 01Safety is one tap away in the bottom bar. The tab gives no clue whether anything needs attention.
  • 02The images show when they were taken. Refresh requests a newer image.
  • 03Spacing and simple icons make the vehicle details easy to scan.

Event review

  • 01Thumbnails show what happened, but urgency is missing. “View all” sits far down the Safety page.
  • 02Filters are easy to find. The small orange risk label sits just above the thumbnail, where it may be missed.
  • 03“Needs Review” is clear and the list is clean. Small colored dots make urgency harder to judge.

Driver information

  • 01Cards separate the driver, vehicle, location, and duty details. The page is long.

  • 02“56,” “down 3,” and “11 events to review” are clear once visible. A longer trend is not shown here.

Design
exploration

I began by defining what each area needed to help the manager do.

08 / 15

What each area needs to show

Home

Fleet status, risky drivers, and action needed — at a glance.

Explore what appears first.

Cameras

Find a vehicle, check its camera and location, then request live video.

Explore video on weak connections.

Events

Filter hundreds of events by driver and behavior; open the clips that matter.

Explore what earns a closer look.

Drivers

See each driver’s score, trend, and repeated behaviors.

Explore how progress or risk stands out.
09 / 15

Design
principles

  1. 01

    Proximity

    Keep related details and actions together.

  2. 02

    Hierarchy

    Use position, size, and labels to make urgency clear.

  3. 03

    Consistency

    Keep labels, imagery, and interactions consistent across pages.

  4. 04

    Load on request

    Let the manager choose when video or map data loads.
